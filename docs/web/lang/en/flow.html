2. Payment

1

1st Payment · Before Ordering

Product cost + domestic shipping + fixed service fee. Complete breakdown provided before payment. No hidden charges.

2

2nd Payment · After Inspection

International shipping, calculated by actual weight/volume. Pay only after you approve the inspection. We support Wise · Bank transfer · Local e-wallets.

Service fee is non-refundable. If the factory is out of stock or cannot ship, product cost is fully refunded; service fee is non-refundable.

3. Inspection

We send photos via WhatsApp within 1 day of warehouse arrival. Please respond within 2 days (1688 return window is only 7 days). No response in 2 days → we follow up once more → still no response → deemed approved. Free storage for 30 days; extension available.

4. Returns & Exchanges

Found an issue? → Send us photos on WhatsApp → Handled as follows:

✅

Factory Error (wrong style/color/quantity)

Return shipping covered by us. We handle it directly with the factory.

✅

Sample Quality Issue (≤5 items + ≤¥300)

Domestic return shipping covered by us. We contact the factory for return and refund.

⚠️

Batch Order Quality Issue

We assist in negotiating with the factory. Domestic return shipping is the buyer's responsibility.

📦

International Shipping Damage

Compensation follows the carrier's standard policy (default: shipping fee refund; insured packages compensated per declared value). We assist with the claim.

❌

Buyer Changed Mind

International + domestic return shipping is the buyer's responsibility. Service fee is non-refundable.

We strongly recommend sampling before bulk orders. Samples: ≤5 items + ≤¥300. Skipping the sample stage and ordering in bulk directly means return/exchange costs are the buyer's responsibility.
