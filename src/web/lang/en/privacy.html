Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we access your Google email address and public display name. When you search for 1688 products, we record the product links you paste. We do not collect your location, browsing history, device information, or use tracking cookies.

How We Use Your Information

Your email is used solely for login identification and order notifications. Your search history is used solely to save analysis results so you can find previous reports in your history. We do not use your data for algorithmic recommendations or advertising.

Who Can See Your Information

Only you and our team. We do not sell your email, search history, or order information to any third party. Not now. Not ever.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in an encrypted database. Your login session exists only in the current browser tab — closing the page automatically signs you out, leaving no trace. We do not transmit your data to anyone.

Your Rights

You can delete your search history at any time. If you want us to completely remove your account and all associated data, contact us via WhatsApp or email — we will process your request within 48 hours.

Cookies

We use Google OAuth for sign-in (which requires browser cookies to complete the Google authentication flow). Beyond that, we do not set any tracking cookies.

Contact Us

Any questions about privacy: Contact page or message us directly on WhatsApp.
