Returns & After-Sales

1688 factories support 7-day unconditional returns. Goods typically arrive at our warehouse within 2–3 days of ordering. We will complete inspection photography and provide feedback within 1 day. Please respond with confirmation or raise any concerns within 2 days to avoid exceeding the 1688 7-day return window.

Return shipping cost rules:
① Factory shipping error (wrong style/color/quantity) → We bear the domestic (China) return shipping cost
② Sample stage (≤5 items per style, total value ≤¥300) with quality issues → We bear the domestic return shipping cost
③ Batch order with quality issues → We assist in negotiating returns/exchanges with the factory; domestic return shipping cost is borne by the buyer

What constitutes a quality issue: color clearly different from order requirements, size deviation exceeding 5%, damage affecting use, material not matching description, missing accessories or insufficient quantity. Minor color variation, normal tolerance deviations in handmade products do not qualify as quality issues. In case of dispute, inspection photos shall prevail, and both parties will negotiate a resolution.

If you do not respond to inspection results within 2 days, we will follow up with a reminder via WhatsApp. If there is still no response, you are deemed to have confirmed approval, and we will proceed with the normal shipping process to avoid missing the 1688 return window.
